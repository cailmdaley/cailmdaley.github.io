I'm an observational cosmologist studying gravitational lensing to map dark matter and constrain cosmology.
I'm a postdoc in the CosmoStat laboratory at CEA Paris-Saclay, the main postdoc on UNIONS cosmic shear and lead of the Euclid × SPT-3G cross-correlation paper, funded by the [TOSCA project](https://tosca.cosmostat.org/).

I'm increasingly drawn to questions at the intersection of AI and scientific practice.
Machine systems are beginning to produce faster than humans can verify—a challenge that touches both alignment and epistemology.
As a core team member of [Lightcone Research](https://lightconeresearch.org), I help build open-source tools for verifiable AI-mediated science.
Some early notes on building democratic infrastructure for machine-speed science can be found [here](/science-ai).
